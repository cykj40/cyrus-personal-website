# Profile Photo Instructions

## Requirements

- **Filename**: avatar.jpg or avatar.png
- **Size**: 800x800px (square/1:1 ratio)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File size**: Under 500KB recommended
- **Quality**: High resolution, well-lit

## Recommendations

- **Background**: Nature setting preferred (forest, mountain, outdoor)
- **Expression**: Confident, approachable, professional
- **Clothing**: Professional but authentic (not overly formal)
- **Framing**: Head and shoulders or upper body
- **Lighting**: Natural light or professional lighting

## How to Add

1. Save your photo to this directory
2. Name it: avatar.jpg or avatar.png
3. The Hero section will automatically display it
